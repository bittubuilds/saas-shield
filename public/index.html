How it works
STEP 1

Register your site

Add your original website. SaaS Shield extracts its public pages conservatively and respects robots.txt.

STEP 2

Add comparisons

Register the competitor or comparison websites you want to check your content against.

STEP 3

Run a scan

The engine compares every eligible page pair using shingle overlap and TF-IDF cosine similarity.

STEP 4

Review matches

See shared passages, scores, and source URLs. Mark findings for review, confirm, or dismiss them.

STEP 5

Export evidence

Download a report with matched excerpts, retrieval timestamps, content hashes, and methodology.
