Content monitoring

Register your original website and comparison sites. SaaS Shield extracts the public pages of each - titles, text, and metadata - with conservative, robots.txt-respecting crawling.

Similarity detection

A deterministic engine compares your pages with comparison pages using shingle overlap, TF-IDF cosine similarity, and matching-passage extraction. Every score is reproducible and derived from real content.

Evidence collection

Each potential match stores both URLs, retrieval timestamps, content hashes, and the matched excerpts. Review it, dismiss it, or export a JSON or PDF report of what was observed.

STEP 1

Add your website

Register your original site and one or more comparison sites in your workspace.

STEP 2

Run a scan

SaaS Shield fetches the public pages and compares every eligible page pair.

STEP 3

Review matches

See shared passages, similarity scores, and source URLs. Mark findings for review, confirm, or dismiss.

STEP 4

Export evidence

Download a report with matched excerpts, methodology, and limitations.

Honest disclosure: SaaS Shield detects potential copying of public web content and records what it observed.
